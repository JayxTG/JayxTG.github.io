Medical Robotics · Swinburne University of Technology · 2024–25

GPU-Accelerated Robotic Ultrasound

Research with the Intelligent Robotics Laboratory at Swinburne University of Technology, published at MERCon 2026. A profiling-driven hybrid CPU–GPU framework for the point-cloud pipeline of an autonomous robotic ultrasound system. Parallel stages such as Taubin smoothing and Fast Global Registration run on the GPU, while ICP stays on the CPU, where naive offloading made it slower.

−27%End-to-end latency, 1.37× speedup

Embodied AI · 2026

RoboBrain 2.0 Extension

Extended the RoboBrain 2.0 vision-language model with multi-turn memory and an agentic LLM planning layer. A complex instruction is split into grounding, affordance and trajectory steps, and 8-bit quantization halves GPU memory for constrained hardware.

−50%GPU memory, 8-bit
PyTorchQwen2.5-VLQuantization

Neurotech · Final-year project · 2026

Pediatric EEG–BCI

An SSVEP brain–computer interface for a child with locked-in syndrome. A ventilator-compatible headset, a custom ADS1299 front end with active electrodes, and FBCCA and TMSI decoding drive an image selector and a maze game.

82%Accuracy, 3 s window

Robotics · Mechatronics · 2024

Pick-and-Place Assembly Robot

A gantry robot that places rivets, washers and transistors onto cylindrical heat sinks for a 3-phase H-bridge. Horizontal and vertical conveyors, a custom gripper and an in-house controller PCB, with an OpenCV tool that centres the gripper over each hole.

UAV Systems · Ongoing

Survey Drone with Aerial Mapping

A survey drone platform for aerial data collection, built by a two-person team under two supervisors. The drone captures images automatically over a survey area, and a reconstruction pipeline stitches them into a map of the surveyed ground.
