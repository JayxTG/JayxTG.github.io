What I’m exploring

UAVs and robots that navigate, perceive, and act reliably outside the lab.

I am now focused on UAVs and robotics that can navigate, perceive, and act reliably outside the lab.

My work combines flight dynamics, embedded electronics, control, and perception systems that learn from changing environments instead of assuming they are clean.

UAV Autonomy

Flight control, navigation, mission planning

Robot Perception

Visual odometry, tracking, mapping, sensing

Embedded Control

STM32, FOC, sensor fusion, real-time systems

Learning-Based Robotics

Manipulation, planning, safe interaction
